What people do with TVs, monitors, radios and cameras like this one:

  • Ham radio, electronics hobby and maker projects.
  • Parts for repairs.

Before you bid on an electronics lot

  • Cables, remotes, stands and power supplies, which are often missing.
  • Whether units were tested. "As-is" with no note usually means untested.
  • The quantity and how it's packed (loose, boxed or on a pallet).

Getting it home. A car or SUV for small lots; bring blankets so screens don't crack.

How to buy it

  1. Open the listing on gsaauctions.gov and read the full description, photos and terms.
  2. Register for a free GSA Auctions account if you don't have one.
  3. Bid online before October 26, 2026. Most items can be inspected in person first; the listing says how.
  4. If you win, pay by the deadline and arrange pickup in Lake City. Items are sold as-is and the buyer handles removal.
