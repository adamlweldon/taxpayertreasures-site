How to buy it

  1. Open Treasury's listing for photos, the floor plan, the inspection dates and the terms of sale.
  2. Register for online bidding with Treasury's auction contractor ahead of time; registration has two steps.
  3. Arrange the required deposit (cashier's check or wire) before the deadline in the listing.
  4. Properties are sold as-is. Inspect in person and check the terms before you bid.
