Before you bid on a generator

  • Its output (kW), voltage and phase. Many military and commercial units can be set for different voltages, but three-phase-only units need a converter for household use.
  • Fuel (diesel, gasoline, natural gas or propane) and hours on the meter.
  • Whether it's on a trailer or a skid, and its weight.
  • Safety: never connect a generator to house wiring without a transfer switch installed by a licensed electrician. Backfeeding the grid can kill utility workers.

Getting it home. Small units fit in a pickup bed. Skid-mounted units need a trailer and a forklift or loader at both ends; trailer-mounted ones need the right hitch (military trailers often use a pintle hook).

How to buy it

  1. Open the listing on gsaauctions.gov and read the full description, photos and terms.
