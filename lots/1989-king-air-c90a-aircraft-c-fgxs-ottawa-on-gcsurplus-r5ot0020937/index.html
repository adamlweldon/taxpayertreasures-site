How to buy it

  1. Open the listing on gsaauctions.gov and read the full description, photos and terms.
  2. Register for a free GSA Auctions account if you don't have one.
  3. Bid online before November 19, 2025. Most items can be inspected in person first; the listing says how.
  4. If you win, pay by the deadline and arrange pickup in Ottawa. Items are sold as-is and the buyer handles removal.
