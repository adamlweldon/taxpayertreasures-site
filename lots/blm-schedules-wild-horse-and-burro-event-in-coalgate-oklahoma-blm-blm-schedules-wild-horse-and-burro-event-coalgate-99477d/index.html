How to take part

  1. Read BLM's adoption requirements first: you need suitable corrals, fencing, shelter and a stock trailer.
  2. Bring a completed application, or apply on site; BLM staff approve adopters before animals leave.
  3. These are untrained wild animals. Plan for gentling and training, or look for a trained horse.
  4. Can't make the event? BLM also lists animals year-round on the Online Corral.
