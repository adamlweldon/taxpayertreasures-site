Privacy policy

Last updated October 17, 2026.

Taxpayer Treasures does not ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, address or payment details. This page explains the limited information that is collected when you visit, and who collects it.

Advertising

We show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on those visits.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings, and you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent, through Google's consent message, before personalized ads are used, and can accept, decline or choose by purpose.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. You can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Email alerts

If you sign up for email alerts, your email address is stored by our newsletter provider and used only to send the alerts you asked for. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them; ads may simply be less relevant.

We don't sell your personal information for money. Some US state privacy laws, such as California's, treat the use of cookies for personalized ads as "selling" or "sharing" personal information, and give you the right to opt out. You can do that at any time with the button below, or the "Your privacy choices" link at the bottom of every page. Visitors in Europe, the UK and Switzerland can use the same button to change or withdraw their consent.

Where your state's law or European law applies, the button opens Google's privacy settings for this site. Elsewhere it opens Google's My Ad Center, where you can turn off personalized ads.

Questions? Contact us.
